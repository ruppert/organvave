An organ with the parts you can hearUm órgão com as partes que você ouve

Every part is ported from setBfree or drawn after the real console's panel: the drawbars, the percussion, the scanner, the rotary and the drive.Cada parte é portada do setBfree ou desenhada conforme o painel do console real: os drawbars, a percussão, o scanner, o rotativo e o drive.

01

Nine drawbarsNove drawbars

Tonewheel tone generator with the key click, the crosstalk and the swell, in integer arithmetic on the FM-1's chip.Gerador de tons de rodas tonais com o click das teclas, o crosstalk e o swell, em aritmética inteira no chip do FM-1.

02

PercussionPercussão

The percussion page, drawn after the console's panel: the harmonic, the decay and the volume of the attack.A página de percussão, desenhada conforme o painel do console: a harmônica, o decay e o volume do ataque.

03

Vibrato and chorusVibrato e chorus

The scanner in three depths, with a chorus mode, and the vibrato selector on the panel.O scanner em três profundidades, com modo chorus, e o seletor de vibrato do painel.

04

Rotary speakerAlto-falante rotativo

The rotary with its speeds and ramps: the horn and the bass drum turn, each at its own rate.O rotativo com as velocidades e as rampas: o corneta e o bumbo giram, cada um na sua taxa.

05

Drive and EQDrive e EQ

Seven drive types, from a clean tube to a hard clip, and a three-band equaliser. Drive at GAIN 0 and EQ at 0 dB are bypassed.Sete tipos de drive, de um tubo limpo a um clip duro, e um equalizador de três bandas. Drive com GAIN 0 e EQ em 0 dB ficam fora do sinal.

06

64 presets and effects64 presets e efeitos

Sixty-four factory presets, and reverb and delay with their send on the panel.Sessenta e quatro presets de fábrica, e reverb e delay com o send no painel.
